# Claims of the technical note

Every bracketed label `[C-xxx]` in the technical note (`notes/technote/main.pdf`, and its Portuguese version
`nota_ptbr.pdf`) refers to a row of this table. Numbers are not consecutive; this is the subset of our working list that
appears in the note.

**Status**
- `proved-lean`: the identity is a theorem in `lean/WheatleyGeom/`, checked by `lake build`. What remains to be trusted is
  that the Lean statement says what the note says.
- `verified-numeric`: computed by a versioned script and, where the Evidence column names a test, covered by an automated
  test in `sim/tests/`. These are floating-point computations, not certified, except where marked. The finite-element
  numbers (C-020) are exploratory simulation outputs, reproducible from the scripts but not covered by a regression test.
- `argued`: a hand-written argument from classical results, or a reading of the literature. It is not formalised and has not
  been checked by a human specialist. Where possible, a numerical test checks its ingredients.

Lean names are theorems in `lean/WheatleyGeom/*.lean`. Test names refer to functions in `sim/tests/`.
